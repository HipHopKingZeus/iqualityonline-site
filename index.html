About Zaher Neket

The man behind I.Quality Online: his picture, his details, his mission, and why he created this business.

The Founder

Zaher Neket, known as Zeus The Prophet

Based in Melbourne, Zaher is a man of faith, an artist, an author, and the host of The Zeus Podcast.

He hosts The Zeus Podcast as Zeus The Prophet, recording on Riverside and publishing to Spotify, YouTube and TikTok, and he releases music as Zeus The Prophet, including The Rise of the Prophet EP and the single Unity.

Everything he creates sits under one umbrella brand: I.Quality Online. Underneath it are The Zeus Podcast, Zeus The Prophet, and The Prophet's Circle.

zeustheprophet.com @zeustheprophet LinkedIn
Mission Statement

To give everything I create one quality home, and to build work that helps people live by truth, faith and family, and be better than they were yesterday.

I.Quality Online exists to publish books, guides, music and conversation that carry a real message: no excuses, only solutions, and growth that starts within and reaches everyone it touches.

Why I Created This Business

One home, built from the ground up

I created I.Quality Online because everything I make was scattered, the podcast in one place, the music in another, the books somewhere else. I built this business so it all has one clean, professional home under one name.

I also built it to last. This brand has to work in the real world, so the books, the guides built from this city, and the community around the work can grow into something that stands on its own for years to come.

And I built it on faith. The Prophet's Circle is built through Christ Consciousness and the love of Jesus Christ, who is my Lord and Savior first and foremost, and that foundation runs through everything this business puts out.

TruthReal talk, no masks, in every episode and every bar.

FamilyEverything here is built to last, and to lift the people it reaches.
